## 1. Overview

The Problem Space PRD established the problem: **a first-time micro-business owner in India cannot easily find out which licenses and registrations their specific business needs — or in what order — so they stall, overpay intermediaries, or operate in fear of penalties.**

This document defines **what we build** to solve it, the boundaries of that build, and the honest 4-day cut (today is 2 Sept; submission is 6 Sept). Solution design that survived grilling; nothing speculative.

The sharpened thesis (from the grill): we are **not** solving "discovery" in the abstract, and we are **not** an AI that answers licensing questions. We are a **curated, city-specific, human-verified rules layer** — *sequenced discovery plus enough hand-holding into each portal that the filing-fear drops* — with a friendly front door. The interface is not the product; **verified accuracy for one city is the product.**

---

## 2. Product thesis & the moat

**Why this isn't just ChatGPT (the load-bearing question).**
By Sept 2026, a digitally literate seller can ask any LLM "what licenses do I need for a café in Ahmedabad" and get a confident, sequenced answer in seconds. If our output *is* that answer, we have nothing. Our only durable advantage:

- **Verified, city-specific, current ground truth** — real Ahmedabad fees, real document lists, real portal deep-links, real dependencies — each carrying an **official source citation and a "verified on <date>" stamp** the UI shows. An LLM cannot show you a dated official citation; it gives national-average mush and hallucinates specifics.
- **A companion, not a document.** A returning user with a saved, trackable checklist ("2 of 5 done") and reminders — not a one-time answer they read and close.
- **A crowdsourced repository of non-happy flows** (real-world rejections, surprise documents, off-book demands) that exists nowhere else on the internet and that an LLM can never have.

**Moat discipline:** the answer content is *never* generated by an LLM. If a rule has no verified official source, it does not appear in the verified spine.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 The wedge
- **Primary persona (design target): P2 — the physical-shop retailer.** Highest license *count* (the bundle + Fire NOC + zoning), highest agent spend, genuinely confused about the *set and order*, and NOT well-served by an LLM because the pain is city-/zoning-specific. We **build for P2's problem** and **test with whoever we can reach** (P3 digital-natives are easiest to instrument first).

### 3.2 The box (in scope, MVP)
- **One city: Ahmedabad (Gujarat).**
- **Three business categories:** small eatery/café · kirana/retail shop · salon. (Chosen because they share the P2 licence bundle — trade/health licence + Shops & Establishment + GST + signage, Fire NOC for eatery — so the rules data overlaps and stays small.)
- **Personalized discovery:** business type + city + scale → the **ordered** set of licences/registrations that apply.
- **Per licence:** what it is · documents needed · government fee · rough timeline · correct sequence/dependencies · **deep link to the correct official portal** · a printable "bring these" document pack.
- **Companion layer:** saveable ordered checklist, mark-done progress, returning-user login + dashboard, time-based reminder nudges.
- **Community field-notes:** clearly-labelled second-tier, user-reported non-happy flows.
- **Bare-bones admin:** internal panel to add/edit/verify rules (no raw DB editing).

### 3.3 Out of scope (this MVP)
- Submitting applications, government backend integration, handling payments/fees.
- **Form-filling / autofill from a user document vault** — *parked, post-MVP or stretch-if-time.* (Kunal's separate idea: a web widget that fills forms from a user-maintained vault of documents. Explicitly a future branch.)
- Automated daily scraping that **overwrites** verified data (see §5 — this is a designed-for pipeline, not an MVP build, and it never silently overwrites).
- Field-note → verified-spine promotion and automated flag-review (designed now, built post-MVP — no data to act on at cold start).
- Nationwide coverage, exhaustive all-licence database, legal advice/guarantees.
- Personas 4 (informal operators regularizing) and 5 (low-digital-literacy / language barrier) — real but harder to serve safely in the timeline.

## 4. Core mechanic — how "which licenses" is decided

**Deterministic curated rules engine is the source of truth. An LLM, if used at all, is only an optional natural-language front door that maps free text onto the questionnaire — it never produces the answer content.**

Flow:
1. User picks a category (eatery/café · retail · salon).
2. A short **branching questionnaire** asks 3–5 disambiguating questions. Examples:
   - **Eatery/café:** seating capacity (Fire NOC threshold) · annual turnover band (GST) · on-premise vs cloud kitchen · alcohol served (y/n).
   - **Retail/kirana:** turnover band (GST) · premises area (signage/trade-licence tier).
   - **Salon:** simplest path — premises + turnover band.
3. Answers key into a **hand-verified rules table** that returns the **ordered** licence set with dependencies.

The moat lives entirely in step 3 being human-verified and cited — not model-generated.

## 5. Data & freshness architecture

Accuracy is the moat, and **accuracy decays** — a manually curated site is trustworthy on day 1 and quietly wrong by day 90, at which point we're worse than an LLM. Freshness is therefore a first-class part of the design, even though most of it is post-MVP.

### 5.1 Two data tiers (never mixed)
- **Verified spine** — curated by us, each rule marked ✓ with `source_url` + `last_verified_date`. This is the trustworthy answer.
- **Community field-notes** — user-submitted non-happy flows, shown as a clearly-labelled second tier ("3 people reported the Ahmedabad office also asked for a rent agreement"). Framed as **field notes, not law.** This makes us *more* credible than an LLM (we distinguish official from street-reality), and it never contaminates the verified spine.

### 5.2 Keeping the spine fresh (post-MVP, schema-ready now)
Automation does the **watching** (tedious, scales); humans keep the **asserting** (the moat).
- **Change-detection watcher (v1.1):** a scheduled job re-fetches each rule's `source_url`, **diffs** against the stored value, and on change **flags the rule "⚠ possibly changed — re-verify"** and notifies ops. It **never silently overwrites** verified data. A human confirms and re-stamps `last_verified_date` via the admin panel.
- **Detecting a brand-new licence** (no government feed exists for this) — three overlapping nets, cheapest→richest:
  1. **Community field-notes = primary early-warning system.** The first real-world signal of a new requirement is a user reporting "the office also asked for a new XYZ permit." A cluster of similar notes auto-flags "investigate: possible new requirement" for human verification → promotion to the spine. **The growth loop and the freshness pipeline are the same loop.**
  2. **Portal notice/circular watching** (secondary) — watch AMC / FoSCoS notification & circular pages for *new links appearing*, not just value changes.
  3. **Periodic manual audit** (backstop) — scheduled human re-review of each category every N months.

### 5.3 MVP reality
MVP ships with **manual curation + citations + date-stamps only.** Scope cap: **~4–6 licences across the 3 categories**, each with a cited official source and a visible verified-on date. Small, sourced, dated, and honestly bounded beats broad and confident-but-wrong. The schema is built so the watcher and field-note promotion can be added without a rewrite.

## 6. Screens & flows

### 6.1 User-facing (7 surfaces + 1 email)
1. **Landing** — one-line value + "Find my licenses" CTA. Also the shareable/LinkedIn top of funnel.
2. **Questionnaire** — category picker → 3–5 disambiguating questions (§4).
3. **Result: ordered licence list** — the payoff. Per licence: what it is · ✓verified + "verified on <date>" + source cite · document checklist · government fee · rough timeline · deep link to official portal. Community field-notes shown as labelled second tier. **No login required to reach here** (value first; also the shareable artefact).
4. **Login / sign-up** — phone-OTP. A real page, serving both first-time save *and* return.
5. **Returning-user home / "My Licenses" dashboard** — the good return experience: saved checklist(s), progress ("2 of 5"), what's pending, resume. This is what makes it a companion, not a document.
6. **Checklist detail** — mark-done + printable "bring these" document pack.
7. **Field-note capture** — a tiny **structured** form (which licence · what happened · what extra doc/step/fee), reached from the reminder email after filing.
8. **Reminder email** (not a screen) — time-based nudge ("saved 3 days ago, 3 still pending — how's it going? anything unexpected?"). Doubles as the re-entry point that harvests field-notes.

### 6.2 Internal
9. **Admin panel (bare-bones, MVP)** — protected route to add/edit licences & rules and set the verify-stamp. All validation happens here — **no raw DB editing.** *Designed-but-post-MVP within the panel:* ⚠flag-review and field-note → spine promotion (no data to act on at cold start).

---

## 7. Auth

**Value first, auth on save.** Anyone runs discovery and sees the full ordered list with **zero login** (also the shareable funnel). Phone-OTP (Supabase) is requested **only** at "save my checklist / remind me" — a fair trade: a phone number for a real benefit (reminders + a saved companion). Returning users log in via the same OTP to reach their dashboard. Satisfies the Playbook's OTP-auth requirement without gating discovery.

---

## 8. Growth & retention loop

The reminder and the field-note are one system, and it feeds the moat:

```
User runs discovery → saves checklist (OTP) → time-based reminder email
   → user returns / reports a non-happy flow (structured field-note)
   → field-notes cluster → human verifies → promoted to verified spine
   → better, fresher product for the next user → (repeat)
```

- **Reminders** = self-declared progress + time-based email nudges (no government status API; out of scope). Email for MVP (free, instant); "get it on WhatsApp" is a manual/stretch touch.
- **Field-notes** = captured **post-filing via the reminder**, structured, per-licence — never an end-of-session survey (nothing real has happened yet at end of session).
- **Cold-start honesty:** the crowd is empty at launch. The MVP loop is **checklist + reminders**; the crowd repo is *seeded* by us (a handful of known gotchas) and *designed-in*, labelled honestly as a post-MVP flywheel. We do not claim a crowd we don't have.

## 9. Tech stack & data model

Per Playbook: **Next.js on Vercel · Supabase (Postgres + phone-OTP auth) · Mixpanel.**

**Rules live as structured data in Supabase from day 1** (not a flat JSON file) — because OTP auth, saved checklists, field-notes, the change-detection watcher and field-note promotion all need a real DB; a config file is a dead-end that forces a rewrite.

Schema (indicative):

| Table | Key fields |
|---|---|
| `licenses` | id, name, description, category, `source_url`, `last_verified_date`, `status` (verified / flagged), govt_fee, rough_timeline, portal_deep_link, required_documents[] |
| `rules` | id, category, condition (answer-key: turnover_band, seating, area, premises_type, …), resulting license_ids[], order/sequence |
| `users` | id, phone, created_at |
| `saved_checklists` | id, user_id, category, answers snapshot, created_at |
| `checklist_items` | id, checklist_id, license_id, status (pending/done) |
| `field_notes` | id, license_id, structured fields (what_happened, extra_doc, extra_fee), reporter (nullable), status (new/reviewing/promoted/rejected), created_at |

## 10. Metrics

**North Star:** number of users who **saved a checklist AND marked ≥1 licence "done."** Tightest proxy for "we changed a real-world outcome" — not vanity engagement.

**Event spine (Mixpanel):**
`flow_started → questionnaire_completed → list_viewed → portal_link_clicked → checklist_saved (OTP) → license_marked_done → field_note_submitted`

This funnel also **ranks the personas** automatically — the thing secondary research could not do.

**Problem-space success signals** (from Problem PRD, still tracked): completes discovery flow · says output told them something new (qualitative) · clicks ≥1 official portal · return/share behaviour.

## 11. Build cut (MoSCoW) — the honest ~4-day plan

Decided cold, now, so it isn't decided in a panic on the 5th.

- **Must-ship** (demo is dead without these): Landing → questionnaire → **verified ordered list with citations + portal links** (screens 1–3) + a bare-bones way to enter the seed rules (minimal admin CRUD, screen 9). This alone is a coherent, defensible product that beats an LLM on the accuracy claim.
- **Should-ship** (fight hard — this is the companion thesis + North Star mechanic): **Login + returning dashboard + checklist mark-done** (screens 4–6).
- **Cut-first, in this order** if time runs short: field-note capture (7) → reminder email (8) → and only then trim the checklist. If field-notes/reminders get cut, we still **design and pitch** the loop in this PRD (it's the vision) — we just don't build it in 4 days.
- **Admin split:** build the bare-bones rule CRUD + verify-stamp now (needed to enter seed rules through the app). Leave flag-review and field-note promotion designed-but-post-MVP.

## 13. Assumptions & open risks

Carried from the Problem PRD (secondary-research-only; primary validation intentionally skipped for this timeline) plus solution-specific:

- **No primary validation.** Persona importance and willingness-to-use are hypotheses; MVP instrumentation ranks them (§10).
- **Accuracy risk** is existential to the moat. Mitigation: scope to one city, cite + date-stamp every verified rule, two-tier data so community reports never contaminate the spine, and honest "verified on <date>" surfacing.
- **"Why not just ChatGPT / an agent / Google?"** Mitigation: verified city-specific dated citations + companion checklist + non-happy-flow repository — none of which an LLM can provide. If we can't deliver *those*, we shouldn't ship.
- **Data freshness.** Mitigation: small sourced rules set now; change-detection watcher (never silent overwrite) + field-note early-warning designed for v1.1.
- **Adoption in 4 days.** Mitigation: value-first (no login gate) shareable list; P3 easiest to reach/instrument first even though we design for P2.
- **Cold-start crowd.** Mitigation: seed known gotchas; MVP loop is checklist+reminders, not the crowd.
